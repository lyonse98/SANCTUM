# Sanctum — Plain-Language Overview

## What This App Is

Sanctum is a private, faith-based companion app for men who are carrying heavy things — anger, addiction, burnout, doubt, grief, pressure at work or at home. When you open it, you don't fill out forms or create an account. You pick one of ten "companions" — wise figures like St. Joseph (the quiet worker and protector), St. Paul (the disciplined fighter), or Job (the man who lost everything and kept faith) — and you simply talk to them. They talk back like a trusted older brother would: direct, honest, grounded in faith, and never preachy.

The app is built around one core promise: **everything stays on your phone.** No account, no email, no name. Your conversations are saved only in your own browser, and you can wipe them instantly with a "panic" button at any time.

## The Ten Companions

Each companion is a different voice for a different kind of struggle:

- **St. Joseph** — for fathers, husbands, and men quietly holding a family together
- **St. Paul** — for discipline, addiction, and starting over after failure
- **King David** — for shame, lust, guilt, and coming back after falling hard
- **St. Peter** — for impulsiveness, screw-ups, and second chances
- **Job** — for loss, grief, and suffering that doesn't make sense
- **Moses** — for reluctant leaders and men who feel unqualified
- **St. John the Baptist** — for simplicity, ego, and cutting through noise
- **St. Leo (the Lion of Rome)** — for courage and moral backbone
- **Elijah** — for burnout, exhaustion, and feeling alone
- **Jonah** — for running from what you know you're supposed to do

You can switch between them anytime. The whole app changes color to match the companion you're talking to.

## Main Features

### 1. Private Conversations
Talk to a companion like texting a friend. Responses stream in instantly. Every companion asks one good question at the end of their reply, because the goal is to get you thinking — not to lecture you. Conversations are capped at short, focused replies (about 120 words) so you get substance, not essays.

### 2. The Home Page — A Daily Ritual
Before you even open a chat, the home screen gives you three things that change daily:
- **Quote of the Day** — wisdom from saints, philosophers (like Marcus Aurelius), and Scripture, rotating every day
- **Saint of the Day** — a real saint whose feast day is today, with a short story of their life, what they struggled with (addiction, doubt, fatherhood, grief...), and — if one of your companions matches them — a one-tap button to start a conversation with that companion
- **Season Badge** — the app knows where the Church calendar is (Advent, Christmas, Lent, Easter, Ordinary Time) and shows it. It even calculates the date of Easter each year automatically. The companions know what season it is too, so they might naturally bring up themes of waiting during Advent, repentance during Lent, or joy at Easter — when it fits the moment.

### 3. The Workshop — 12 Tools That Work Offline
These are practical exercises that open instantly and need no internet:
- **Cold Start** — a 60-second interrupter for anger or panic
- **Breath Pacer** — guided breathing (box breathing, 4-7-8) to calm your body
- **5-4-3-2-1 Grounding** — a sensory exercise to pull you out of a spiral
- **Evening Examen** — St. Ignatius's five-step review of your day
- **Rule of Life** — a daily habit tracker for prayer and discipline
- **Prayer Library** — the creeds and classic prayers
- **Rosary** — a guided rosary with the day's mysteries
- **Bible in a Year** — a full 365-day reading plan
- **Confession Guide** — an examination of conscience
- **Pocket Catechism** — the key teachings of the faith
- **Driveway Reset** — a 90-second ritual to leave work stress in the car before you walk in the house
- **NSDR / Sleep** — a deep-rest body scan with Scripture

Your companions know about these tools. If you tell St. Paul you're about to relapse, he can hand you a button that opens the Cold Start tool right in the conversation.

### 4. Real Crisis Support
If a man mentions suicide or self-harm, the app stops everything and immediately shows real help: the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, the Crisis Text Line, the Veterans Crisis Line, the Domestic Violence Hotline, and international options. The app never pretends to be a therapist — for mental health it points to licensed counselors.

### 5. Extras
- **Incognito mode** — chat without anything being saved at all
- **Share cards** — turn a quote or moment into a shareable image
- **A connected newsletter** ("The Daily Battle Order") — mentioned only if you're interested, never pushed on you

## How It Works Under the Hood (Without the Jargon)

When you send a message, it travels to a small, private relay server that:
1. Checks that nothing about the request can be tampered with
2. Applies the safety rules — the "be direct but kind, keep it short, always end with a question, never give medical advice" rulebook that lives on the server where nobody can strip it out
3. Limits how many messages any one person (or machine) can send, using a privacy-safe method — it never records who you are or where you are; even the technical identifier is scrambled beyond recovery
4. Forwards your conversation to the AI engine and streams the answer back to you

The relay server holds the secret key to the AI. Your phone never sees it, and your conversations are never stored on the server — the only thing it ever keeps is a scrambled, unrecoverable identifier plus a message count.

## What's Stored, and Where

- **Your chats, settings, and daily streak:** on your own device, in your own browser's storage
- **The relay server:** a scrambled machine identifier and message counts only — no names, no emails, no message content
- **Delete everything:** the panic button wipes all history instantly; clearing your browser does the same

## What's Built but Not Switched On Yet

Two features are pre-built for the future:
1. **Audio Library** — a full listening section (guided Rosary, meditations, breathing exercises, Psalm readings) is designed and ready; it just needs the audio files recorded and dropped in
2. **Brotherhood Circles** — small groups of 2–5 men who could share prayer intentions and check in on each other. The database structure is ready and locked tight; it activates when accounts are added

## The Design Philosophy in One Sentence

A man should be able to open this app at his lowest moment, in a parking lot or at 2 a.m., tell the truth to someone wise, get a real answer and one small next step, and know that when he leaves, nothing about him is left behind.
